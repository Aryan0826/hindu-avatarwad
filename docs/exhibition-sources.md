# Exhibition source locators

Verified 7 November 2026. The visitor-facing references name ancient scriptures, not the website or modern edition used to inspect them. Links below are developer research locators only; the exhibit panels contain no hyperlinks. Accounts and takeaways are original exhibition summaries and interpretations.

| Avatar | Shaastra reference shown |
| --- | --- |
| Matsya | Shrimad Bhagavata Purana, Skandha 8, Chapter 24 |
| Kurma | Shrimad Bhagavata Purana, Skandha 8, Chapters 6–9; especially 8.7.6–10 |
| Varaha | Shrimad Bhagavata Purana, Skandha 3, Chapters 13, 18–19 |
| Narasimha | Shrimad Bhagavata Purana, Skandha 7, Chapters 5–10; manifestation in Chapter 8 |
| Vamana | Shrimad Bhagavata Purana, Skandha 8, Chapters 18–23; Bali’s surrender in Chapter 22 |
| Parashurama | Shrimad Bhagavata Purana, Skandha 9, Chapters 15–16; forgiveness in 9.15.40 |
| Shri Rama | Shrimad Bhagavata Purana, Skandha 9, Chapter 10; exile in verses 4 and 8, Ravana’s defeat in verse 23 |
| Shri Krishna | Shrimad Bhagavata Purana, Skandha 10, Chapters 25 and 44; Bhagavad Gita 2.47 and 4.7–8 |
| Buddha | Shrimad Bhagavata Purana 1.3.24 and 11.4.22; Bhagavad Gita 16.1–3 for the explicitly related Hindu value of ahimsa |
| Kalki | Shrimad Bhagavata Purana, Skandha 12, Chapter 2, verses 18–24 |

## Online Sanskrit and translation locators

- https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/8/24/ — Matsya, preservation through the flood and recovery of the Vedas.
- https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/8/7/ — Kurma supporting Mandara; verses 6–10 describe the sinking and support. Broader context: Chapters 6–9.
- https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/3/13/ and https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/3/19/ — Varaha’s manifestation, rescue of Earth and defeat of Hiranyaksha; Chapter 18 supplies the preceding encounter.
- https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/7/8/ — Narasimha’s manifestation and protection of Prahlad. Chapters 5–10 give persecution, prayer and blessing.
- https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/8/22/ — Bali’s surrender and protection in Sutala. Chapters 18–23 give the full Vamana account.
- https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/9/15/ and https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/9/16/ — Parashurama; Jamadagni’s teaching on forgiveness is 9.15.40. This is not presented as an instruction to imitate violence.
- https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/9/10/ — Rama’s birth, exile, Ravana’s defeat and return to Ayodhya. This replaces modern Ramayan publication recommendations on the exhibit.
- https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/10/25/ and https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/10/44/ — Krishna’s protection at Govardhan and defeat of Kamsa.
- https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/2/47/ , https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/4/7/ and https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/4/8/ — Krishna’s teaching on action and manifestation; 4.7–8 also supports the retained opening.
- https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/1/3/24/ and https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/11/4/22/ — Buddha in the Bhagavata Purana. The ahimsa takeaway is explicitly labelled a related Hindu value, from https://vedabase.io/en/library/bg/16/1-3/ , rather than a claimed direct quotation from Buddha.
- https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/12/2/ — Kalki as future prophecy; no portrait or calendar prediction is supplied.

VedaBase is a locator for the texts and a particular translation/commentary tradition, not an additional shaastra. The display does not claim that every Hindu tradition uses the same list of ten avatars. Existing concise accounts are retained with the references above; modern BAPS publication recommendations were removed in accordance with this revision’s source restriction.
